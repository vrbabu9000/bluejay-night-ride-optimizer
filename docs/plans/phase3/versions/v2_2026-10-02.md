## Why v2
v1 built synthetic nights (demand, fleet, a 330-scenario grid) before anyone had written down how the real service runs. On review (2 Oct) the team asked for three things first:
1. how TransLoc and the Hopkins dispatch actually operate: who assigns rides, whether drivers accept or reject, how Lyft overflow happens;
2. an agreed definition of what a scenario contains, built from (1) and agreed **before** any data is generated, starting small (2 vans, 16 rides) and adding complexity step by step;
3. road times and routes from a local, pinned OSRM instead of the public demo server.

## What carries over from v1 (reused, not rebuilt)
- **Locations and zones:** `locations_v1.csv`, `zones_v1.csv`, depot rule D-17.
