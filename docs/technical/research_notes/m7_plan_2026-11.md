### 3a. Power pilot (runs first)
- **Pairs:** R0 and R3(2), 5 vans, on the 10 nights **base_5 r30–r39**. These are outside the main 30 (prereg v2 §4). They were run only as M5d first hours under R0, with C, C-S6 and G.
- **Expected half-width:** for the primary measure, the 30-night expected 95% half-width is computed as `1.96 × sd(R3(2) − R0 per night, ddof = 1) / √30`.
- **Rule:**
  - If it is ≤ 0.7 δ = 1.4 pp, the main grid uses **30 nights**.
  - Otherwise it uses **60 nights**.
  - At 60 nights, the same formula with √60 is reported. If it still exceeds 1.4 pp, every pair is reported as "underpowered at δ" and no further nights are added.
- **Other measures:** the same half-widths are reported for P90 wait (0.7 δ = 0.7 min) and bookings per 100 (0.7 δ_B = 1.4), but they do not set the night count.
- **Outcomes:** the pilot's outcomes are used for nothing else. They are not pooled into the main analysis.

### 3b. Main grid
- **30 nights:** base_5 and base_6, **r00–r29**.
- **60 nights:** r00–r29 plus **r40–r69**, skipping the pilot nights r30–r39.
  - base_5 r40–r59 already exist (built 18 Nov).
  - base_5 r60–r69 and base_6 r40–r69 are built by the same builder and seed scheme (`src/scenario/build_l6_v2.py`). The fresh replicates are new independent draws, and the 6-van nights reuse the 5-van requests (D-37).
  - Every existing L6 hash must rebuild unchanged.
- **Runs:** 6 rules × 2 fleets × the nights.
- **Order:** night by night (all 12 rule × fleet runs of r00, then r01, …), so that a stopped grid leaves complete nights.
- **Workers:** up to 8. Peak memory per full evening is ≤ 1.1 GB (M6), on 10 cores and 16 GB.
- **Expected compute:** about 9 min per run.
  - 30 nights: 360 runs, about 7 h.
  - 60 nights: 720 runs, about 14 h.
- **Cutoff:** **30 Nov 2026 12:00 ET.** Runs finishing later are not used.
- **Nights that count:**
  - the longest unbroken prefix of the night list in which every one of the 12 runs finished by the cutoff (any check status);
  - minus nights where any run failed a check *without an explanation recorded under §3c*.
  - Both counts are reported.

### 3c. Failed checks
- **Crashes:** a crash pauses the grid. The cause is found before resuming.
- **Failed run checks:** a failed check does not pause. Each failure is diagnosed and recorded in the M7 run record before the analysis runs.
- **Explained failure:** a night is kept only if the failure is shown to be the known guardrail-attribution limit found in M6 (24 Nov, M6 r02). That limit is:
  - an unavoidable excess, after a van no-show, shared between two or more riders aboard by the dispatcher's least-total-violation choice;
  - shown by recomputing the alternative drop orders, as in the M6 record.
- **Any other failure:** the night is excluded.
- **Sensitivity:** the analysis is also reported with every failed-check night excluded.

## 4. Measures and comparisons (prereg v2 §3–§4, unchanged)
- **Primary:** the share of all requests not reached by a vehicle (curb arrival, van or Lyft) within 20 min. Cancelled riders count as not reached. δ = 2 pp.
- **Secondary:** passenger P90 wait among riders picked up. δ = 1 min.
- **Resource:** Lyft bookings per 100 requests, standard and XL. δ_B = 2.
- **Sensitivity:** the primary share at 15 min.
- **Descriptive:** van-km, the spec §9.3 explanation measures available from the logs, and cancellations.
- **Comparisons:**
  - for each fleet, all 15 pairs of rules;
  - paired per-night differences;
  - 95% percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples of nights, seed 20261110, one common resample index for every pair and measure (as in `sim.intervals`).
- **Readings** (D = A − B; larger is worse):
  - A is **better** if the upper bound < 0;
  - A is **non-inferior** if the upper bound < +δ;
  - an interval containing 0 means "cannot tell".
- **Dominance (D-50 amendment 1):** A dominates B if A is non-inferior on all three measures and better on at least one. Otherwise both are on the frontier, and the trade-off is reported. δ_B = 1 is also reported as a sensitivity.
- **Exploratory:** everything in this section is exploratory, at 95%.

## 5. H3, the one confirmatory test
- **Statement (prereg v2):** "With 6 vans, the differences between rules shrink relative to 5 vans."
- **Per-night contrast (fixed now):** for night n and transfer rule A ∈ {R1(10), R1(15), R1u(10), R2(15), R3(2)}, let
  e_A,f(n) = primary(A, f vans, n) − primary(R0, f vans, n).
  The H3 contrast is the mean over the five rules of **|e_A,5(n)| − |e_A,6(n)|** (pp).
  - It is positive when rule effects relative to R0 are smaller with 6 vans.
  - The two fleets share requests (D-37), so the contrast is paired by night.
- **Test:** a 98.3% percentile bootstrap over nights (10,000 resamples, seed 20261110). **H3 is supported** if the lower bound > 0. It is **contradicted** if the upper bound < 0. Otherwise it is **not settled**.
- **Reported beside it (exploratory, 95%):**
  - the per-rule contrast |e_A,5| − |e_A,6|;
  - the same contrast on P90 wait and bookings per 100.

## 6. What is reported
- **Compute:** per run, the wall time, peak memory, re-plans, solver status and checks. This is the M7 run record.
- **Per fleet:**
  - the six rules' means;
  - the 15 pair intervals on the three measures, plus the 15-min sensitivity;
  - the dominance table at δ_B = 2 and δ_B = 1;
  - the frontier (not reached within 20 min against bookings per 100).
- **H3:** the result against §5.
- **Traced night:** one evening shown end to end under R0 and one transfer rule (spec §9.3: 2–3 traced nights). **r00** under R0 and R2(15), 5 vans, fixed now.
- **Analysis code:** `python -m sim.m7 compare`. It is written and tested on a self-comparison before the main grid runs. Any later analysis is a new, labelled analysis.
