## Checks
1. **Hour counts: pass.** Hand calculation (spec §3.2):
   - base: 5 × ρ = 64.25, 50.50, 53.55, which rounds to a total of 168. The floors sum to 167, and the leftover unit goes to the largest fraction (.55), giving **64 / 50 / 54**;
   - high: × 1.3 = 83.525, 65.65, 69.615, total 219. The floors sum to 217, and the two leftover units go to .65 and .615, giving **83 / 66 / 70**.

   Every one of the 90 nights has exactly these counts, and its first-hour rows are exactly the first n₀ rows.
2. **Draws follow the recipe: pass.** Pooled over 30 nights (5,040 base and 6,570 high requests):
   - party shares are within |z| ≤ 1.9 of .73 / .18 / .055 / .025 / .01;
   - origin-zone χ² is 20.7 (base) and 14.8 (high) on 15 df; destination-zone χ² is 8.6 and 12.0;
   - patience median is 1,077 / 1,084 s (recipe 1,080);
   - Lyft delay median is 485 / 481 s (recipe 480) and P90 is 898 / 908 s (recipe 900);
   - the uniform draws have mean 0.50.

   One base origin zone (Z03) is 3.0 standard errors below its expected share (5.6% vs 6.7%). With 16 zones that is not unusual, and the overall χ² (p ≈ 0.15) does not flag it. It is recorded, not corrected. Full table: `outputs/scenario/l6_check.md`.
3. **Reproducible: pass.** Rebuilding nights in memory reproduces the frozen hashes. Changing one component's stream (patience) changes only that column.
4. **Pairing: pass.**
   - base_6 has byte-identical `requests.csv` and `latent.csv` to base_5, so only availability differs.
   - Base and high nights with the same replicate number are different nights, by design (D-49): no comparison is paired across patterns.
5. **Map: pass.** `outputs/scenario/l6_map_base_r00.png` shows requests concentrated in Homewood, the Peabody and downtown tail to the south, and the depot central.
