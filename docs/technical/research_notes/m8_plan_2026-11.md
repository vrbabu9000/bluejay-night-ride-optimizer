# M8 plan: sensitivity of the Lyft-rule results to rider patience, Lyft arrival time and acceptance

**Status: FROZEN 2026-11-25**

**Why:** Prof. Kearsley's review (25 Nov 2026) asked for "a sensitivity analysis examining how the results change when the assumed rider patience distribution, Lyft arrival times, and acceptance rates are changed". All three are ASSUMED placeholders (generator notes; spec AS-08; P-08).
**Decision:** D-66 (proposed; the team approves).
**Written:** 25 Nov 2026, after the M7 results, which are therefore known. That is why M8 is a **sensitivity analysis with no confirmatory test**. Everything below is fixed before any M8 run.

## 1. What M8 answers
1. Do the M7 five-van findings hold when each assumption is moved?
   - (a) every transfer rule that fires reaches more riders than R0;
   - (b) the predicted-wait rules (R2(15), R3(2)) gain more per Lyft booking than the elapsed-wait rule R1(10).
2. How large does each rule's effect become, and how does its booking cost change?

It does **not** test H1–H4 again, and it calibrates nothing. The settings are plausible ranges, not estimates.

## 2. Settings (one at a time, around the M7 base)
| Setting | Changed | How | Base value |
|---|---|---|---|
| `patience_short` | rider patience | every rider's frozen draw × 2/3 (Weibull median 18 → 12 min) | median 18 min |
| `patience_long` | rider patience | × 3/2 (median 18 → 27 min) | |
| `lyft_fast` | Lyft arrival delay after booking | every rider's frozen draw × 2/3 (lognormal median 8 → 5.3 min) | median 8 min, P90 15 |
| `lyft_slow` | Lyft arrival delay | × 3/2 (median 8 → 12 min) | |
| `consent_80` | acceptance of a Lyft offer | p_c = 0.8 (a rider accepts if their frozen draw u < p_c) | p_c = 1.0 |
| `consent_60` | acceptance | p_c = 0.6 | |

- **Common random numbers:**
  - Patience and delay are scaled from each rider's frozen draw, so the riders, times, places and party sizes are identical across settings.
  - Acceptance uses the frozen u, so the riders who decline at 0.8 also decline at 0.6.
- **What a decline means:** the rider stays with the vans (engine, as in L4).
- **The rules' own assumption is not changed.** R3(2) still compares against its built-in typical Lyft delay of 8 min (lyft.py). Under `lyft_fast` and `lyft_slow`, that assumption is therefore wrong. That is intended: it is what a fixed rule would face.

## 3. Runs
- **Grid:** five vans, base pattern, L6 nights r00–r29 (the M7 main nights), full evenings, C exactly as in M7, code fingerprint `1f318cef8ad4`.
- **Rules:** R1(10), R1(15), R2(15), R3(2) in every setting. **R0** is run in the patience settings only.
  - Under the Lyft and acceptance settings, R0 is the M7 base run. R0 makes no booking, so Lyft delay and acceptance cannot change it. A test confirms this on a real run.
  - **R1u(10) is not run:** it made 0 transfers on all 60 M7 evenings (`m7_results_2026-11-24.md` §3).
- **Base:** the M7 five-van runs (same nights, fingerprint and settings), not re-run.
- **Size:** 2 × 5 + 4 × 4 = 26 runs per night; 780 in all. The expected compute is about 20 h at 8 workers.
- **Order:** night by night. A prefix of complete nights exists at any time.
- **Cutoff:** **28 Nov 2026 12:00 ET.**
  - The analysis uses the longest prefix r00…rk of nights with every M8 run finished before the cutoff.
  - At least 10 nights are needed for any interval to be reported; otherwise only means are shown, labelled.
- **Crashes and failed checks:** handled as M7 §3c.
  - A crash pauses the grid.
  - A failed guardrail-attribution check that `sim.m7_explain` explains keeps its night.
  - Any other failed check drops that night from every setting, and the strict version is reported too.
- **Runner:** `python -m sim.m8 run --workers 8`. It refuses an unfrozen plan or another fingerprint, and it labels each manifest with the setting, the transform and this plan's SHA-256.

## 4. Measures and analysis (fixed now)
- **Measures:** the M7 measures: not reached within 20 min (primary), within 15 min, P90 passenger wait, and Lyft bookings per 100. Added for this purpose: the share of transferred riders who cancel before the Lyft arrives.
- **Effect** of rule A in setting s, per night: E = A_s − R0_s (R0_s = R0_base for the Lyft and acceptance settings).
  - Reported with a paired percentile bootstrap over nights at 95% (10,000 resamples, seed 20261110), as in M7.
- **Shift from base:** per night, E_s − E_base, with the same bootstrap at 95%.
- **Gain per booking:** mean over nights of (R0_s − A_s) on the primary measure, divided by the mean bookings per 100 of A_s. Interval from the same resamples (ratio of the resampled means).
- **Robustness readings (descriptive, no multiplicity adjustment):**
  - (a) *holds* in setting s for rule A if the 95% upper bound of E_s is below 0;
  - (b) *holds* in setting s if the point estimate of gain per booking of both R2(15) and R3(2) exceeds R1(10)'s, and is reported with its intervals.
  - Anything else is reported as it is.
- **No new dominance tables:** the M7 tie question (D-65 §8) is open, and M8 is not the place to settle it. The frontier points are shown per setting.

## 5. What M8 does not show
- Calibrated values.
- Joint changes (the settings move one at a time).
- Busy, far-trip or high-load nights.
- Six vans.
- Real Lyft behaviour (no data).
- Any recommendation.
