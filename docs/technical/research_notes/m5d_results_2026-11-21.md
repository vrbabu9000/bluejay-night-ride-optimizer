## How to read it
- **The switch costs no riders that matter.** At high load, C-S6 and C leave essentially the same share unreached within 20 min (33.0% and 32.9% on average). Of the 30 nights, C-S6 was worse on 7, better on 5 and tied on 18. The interval sits well inside the ±2 pp margin. Base load shows the same pattern: 27 ties and 3 nights worse.
- **The diagnostic's prediction held on new nights.** The threshold of 6 was chosen on r00–r29. The fresh nights confirm it, rather than reflecting a fit to the nights it was chosen on.
- **The compute tail shrinks, but not enough.** At high load the median falls from 58 to 38 min and the worst night from 6.4 h to 2.1 h (3×). Peak memory falls from 5.9 to 3.8 GB. The median still misses the 15-min gate by more than double, as the preregistration expected. The gate is not reinterpreted.
- **C still beats greedy on fresh nights.** This is a descriptive replication of M5b on new nights:
  - C − G: high load −10.72 pp [−12.73, −8.80]; base −7.60 pp [−8.85, −6.30];
  - C-S6 − G: −10.60 pp at high load and −7.34 pp at base.
  - The M5b base_5 primary result was −6.35 pp [−8.65, −4.17], so the fresh base nights land in the same place.
- **Secondary measures, C-S6 − C** (exploratory, no decision rests on them): P90 wait, P90 ride and van-km per ride all differ by less than 0.1 min or 0.1 km. Cancellations per night rise by +0.13 at both loads. At base load that interval excludes 0 ([+0.03, +0.27]), but it amounts to about one cancellation in eight nights.
